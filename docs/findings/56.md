## Part 2 — the check 53a
asserted and did not
report

The clause: a criterion
is not another
criterion's mechanism. A
named way one lettered
criterion may be
satisfied does not
become a second letter.
A mechanism that
protects something the
other letter does not
require may still be
admitted — as that
something, not as the
way.

Applied to C1 through
C16. Newly excludes
none. The clause was
not sharpened. A rule
that excludes nothing
retroactively is a rule
that was written
narrowly. Worth knowing.

**C8 and C15 are the
shape the clause is
about.** C15's
compiled-absence is how
C8's rejection branch
has effect in the
reference. C15 is the
placement — authorisation
layer, not the gateway —
which C8 does not
require. The engine's
clause list states C8 as
explicitly not the
authorisation-server
half of C15. A denylist
would satisfy C8 and
fail C15. Both stay.

**C13 and C12 are
different facts.** C12
is an assignment in
force for a governed
operation. C13 is that
an assignment cannot be
applied to a composition
other than the one it
was computed from.
Wrong composition and
missing assignment are
not the same defect.
Digest binding serves a
property that is not
another letter. Both
stay.

C16 and C11 remain as
53a stated. C2 and C12
remain different facts:
undeclared versus no
assignment. C1 and C10
share precedence and
are not one the other's
mechanism. C13 and C10:
the record carries a
digest; C13 is the
discipline of that
digest.

Nothing already lettered
would be excluded. That
is not a tidy-up. It is
the result of the check.
